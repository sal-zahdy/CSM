Best Practices — CSM Rituals
01

Segmented Reporting

Same data, three different stories: Operations, Finance, and Executive. Each audience gets exactly what they need to see value.

02

Health Score

A monthly early-warning system before the account turns red. Proactive risk management before silent churn takes hold.

03

Value Log

Every prevented failure converted into EGP value. If value is invisible, renewal is a debate. If value is visible, renewal is logical.

04

QBR with Decision-Maker

30 minutes with the person who controls renewal — every quarter, without exception. No renewal without a decision-maker in the room.
